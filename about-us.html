Our Origin

Built by people who live on the show floor

LV Trade Show Rentals started in 2004 as a small install-and-dismantle crew at the Las Vegas Convention Center. Two decades later we're a full-service exhibit house — but that hands-on, get-it-done DNA hasn't changed.

  • 20+ years exhibiting across Las Vegas venues
